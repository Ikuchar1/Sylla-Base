Instructor-provided files go here: slide decks, handouts, study guides, the syllabus,
the textbook.

Name them so they sort and match your notes:

    01-intro.pdf
    2026-08-27-intro-to-fronts.pdf
    exam-1-study-guide.pdf

Prefer PDF over .pptx — Claude reads PDFs directly; a .pptx loses figures and order.

**Textbooks:** drop in the full PDF. On first ingest Claude extracts it once to
`<book>-full.txt` plus `<book>-chapter-index.md` (chapter → line range) and greps that —
it never splits the PDF.
